This is the canonical Paper IV source directory. The manuscript extends the separated-weight interpolation-determinant method to algebraic exponential certificates with compatible conjugate fibres. It proves the bound $2d/r$ and develops certificate optimization, power saturation, torsion-defect, and relation-lattice corollaries.

The paper does not claim exponent 2 for every algebraic exponential fibre.
