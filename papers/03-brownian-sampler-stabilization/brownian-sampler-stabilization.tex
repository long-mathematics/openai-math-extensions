\begin{abstract}
OpenAI's Brownian factor-of-IID sampler transforms independent Brownian
innovations into a path field whose joint law with its binary output $X$ is
that of
\[
  Y_i(t)=tX_i+B_i(t),
\]
where the $B_i$ are independent standard Brownian motions independent of
$X$.  We analyze the continuous-time midpoint readout
$\widehat X_i(t)=\1\{Y_i(t)>t/2\}$.  If $T_i$ is the supremum of the times at which
coordinate $i$ is decoded incorrectly, then, conditionally on the entire
sampled configuration, the variables $T_i$ are independent with common law
$4\chi_1^2=\GammaLaw(1/2,8)$.  In particular, the stabilization-time field
is independent of the sampled configuration.  We obtain the exact finite-set
law
\[
  \P\!\left(\max_{i\in A}T_i\le t\right)
  =\left[1-2\Phi\!\left(-\frac{\sqrt t}{2}\right)\right]^{|A|},
\]
an exact binomial law for the remaining terminal defects, explicit confidence
horizons, and a Gumbel limit with centering
$8\log M-4\log\log M-4\log\pi$ and scale $8$.  We also prove moment
asymptotics, unequal-rate and deterministic-clock versions, and a quantitative
Poisson transfer principle for heterogeneous coordinates.  For finite
target laws we also identify the sampler drift through the vector I--MMSE
identity, obtaining an exact entropy-dissipation formula.  The results apply,
in particular, to the free uniform spanning forest sampler constructed in the
OpenAI preprint.
\end{abstract}
