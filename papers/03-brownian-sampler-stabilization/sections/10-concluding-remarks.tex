\section{Concluding remarks}

The OpenAI construction supplies an exact observation representation, while
the midpoint rule turns the two target states into the same negative-drift
Brownian last-passage problem.  This yields more than a marginal tail estimate:
it produces an independent gamma marking of the entire sampled configuration.
For a finite family, permanent stabilization is the zero-defect event in an
exact binomial process.  The Gumbel law is consequently the zero-count limit of
an explicit Poisson terminal-defect field.

The result is insensitive to the dependence structure of the sampled binary
process.  All model-specific work is confined to establishing the Brownian
sampler representation.  Once that representation holds, the stabilization
law, its extreme-value limit, and its deterministic-clock extensions are
universal.
