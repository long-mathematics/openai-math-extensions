\section{Clause omission}
\label{sec:omission}

Fix $\rho<\infty$, let $1\le L\le\rho n$, and put
\[
    T=H_n\wedge L.
\]
For each $1\le i\le L$, omit the clause at index $i$ while retaining the
original indices:
\[
    F_m^{(-i)}
    =\bigwedge_{\substack{1\le j\le m\\j\ne i}} C_j.
\]
Let $T^{(-i)}$ be the first index at which this omitted-clause process is
unsatisfiable, capped at $L$, and set
\[
    D_i=T^{(-i)}-T\ge0.
\]
The use of the original indices is important because the clause laws may
depend on time.

\begin{lemma}[Coordinate omission]
\label{lem:coordinate-omission}
The capped hitting time satisfies
\begin{equation}
    \Var(T)\le\sum_{i=1}^L\E D_i^2.
    \label{eq:coordinate-omission}
\end{equation}
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
This is the coordinate-omission form of the Efron--Stein inequality; see
\citet{EfronStein1981} and \citet[Section~3.2]{BBLM2005}.  For completeness,
let
\[
    \cF_i=\sigma(C_1,\dots,C_i),
    \qquad
    \Delta_iX=\E[X\mid\cF_i]-\E[X\mid\cF_{i-1}].
\]
Because the clause coordinates are independent and $T^{(-i)}$ does not
depend on $C_i$, one has $\Delta_iT^{(-i)}=0$.  Therefore
$\Delta_iT=-\Delta_iD_i$.  Conditional expectation is an orthogonal
projection in $L^2$, so
\[
    \E(\Delta_iD_i)^2\le\E D_i^2.
\]
Summing the orthogonal martingale differences of $T$ proves
\eqref{eq:coordinate-omission}.
\end{proof}

It remains to prove a uniform bound on $\E D_i^2$.  We do this after
conditioning on the value of the omitted clause.
