\begin{abstract}
For the ferromagnetic four-state Potts broadcast process on an arbitrary
locally finite deterministic rooted tree, suppose the edge-dependent
second eigenvalues satisfy $0<\lambda_e\le q<1$. We show that
reconstruction occurs if and only if the associated $L^3$ nonlinear
capacity is positive, with path weights given by the products of squared
edge eigenvalues. Neither a uniform vertex-degree bound nor a positive
lower bound on the channel parameters is required. We establish sharp
comparability, up to constants depending only on the uniform contraction
bound, between quadratic root information and capacity; total-variation
advantage then follows by the optimal elementary conversions. Robust
reconstruction is equivalent to the same capacity condition. We also derive
an exact series criterion for spherically symmetric trees, including at the
Kesten--Stigum critical boundary. The proof rests on the
recent four-state posterior moment-saving inequality. A
cubic correction converts that input into a degree-free nonlinear flow
recursion. Conversely, an elementary two-moment calculation, a controlled
independent erasure, and an exact flow-averaged path identity give
reconstruction from positive capacity without invoking the imported
inequality. Beyond the uniformly contractive regime we prove a necessary
criterion in terms of an edge-noise-weighted capacity, which is exact on one
ray; the full weighted-capacity converse remains open.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords.} Broadcasting on trees; four-state Potts model;
reconstruction; nonlinear capacity; heterogeneous channels; information flow.
