\section{Dependence on computationally certified input}
\label{sec:provenance}

The only imported inequality in the proof of
Theorems~\ref{thm:main} and \ref{thm:weighted-necessity} is
Proposition~\ref{prop:input}, the preserved-class moment-saving estimate of
\cite[Proposition~3.2]{OAIThreshold}. The citation is pinned to OpenAI/math
commit \texttt{fd4aeeb2ee4fc729c18d98444fed42fd0529eeeb}. That source proves
its underlying polynomial inequalities with exact rational coefficient
certificates, subdivision, and executable verifiers; see its certificate and
finite-verification sections. Consequently, the two necessity arguments are
conditional only in the ordinary sense that they invoke this explicitly
stated theorem of the cited preprint. The sufficiency implication, robust
reconstruction corollary, and their quantitative lower bounds are independent
of that imported result.

All remaining estimates, including the universal product identities, the
sharpened coefficient count, the cubic subadditivity argument, the weighted
flow recursion, and the heterogeneous path summation, are established in the
present paper. The exact integer expansion used in
Lemma~\ref{lem:local} is independently checked by
\texttt{verify\_local\_expansion.py} in the source directory.
